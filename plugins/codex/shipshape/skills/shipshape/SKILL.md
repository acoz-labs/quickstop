---
name: shipshape
description: Shape an assigned outcome, preserve local policy, and preflight a bounded delivery contract.
---

Read [runtime obligations](../../references/runtime.md) and
[operator protocol](../../references/operator.md) from this installed directory.
Inspect the assigned issue, local instructions and existing `.sdlc/config.json`.
Guide-only adoption is valid. A read-only request yields a proposal only.
Do not adopt, upgrade, configure services or obtain credentials automatically.
Pin the immutable identities and bounded effects before implementation. Missing
tools, auth, reviewer or sandbox are blockers. Use the package-relative
[helper](../../scripts/shipshape.py) for advisory preflight, never as approval.
Route authorized code work to shipshape-implement and fresh independent review
to shipshape-review. Preserve the explicit stopping point on resumption.
