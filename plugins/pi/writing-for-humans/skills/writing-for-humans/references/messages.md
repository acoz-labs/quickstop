# Slack and email

Lead with the news or ask. Supply enough local context for recipients to understand its consequence, then add a useful link or supporting detail. State timing and responsible people only when established. Match the destination's formatting and expected depth; a short message need not acquire a document's headings.

A link should support the message rather than carry its meaning. Preserve uncertainty, quotations and explicit voice preferences. Avoid repeating an investigation diary or claiming consensus that the supplied facts do not establish. Improve the authorized draft; the writing skill itself grants no permission to send it.
