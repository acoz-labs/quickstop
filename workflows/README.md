# Workflows

Practical guides you and your agents can read and adapt to your own projects.
Unlike [plugins](../docs/catalog.md), workflows are ordinary documentation:
there is nothing to install, enable or keep running.

| Workflow | What it helps with | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shipshape** | Taking software from an initial idea to a verified result, with process proportional to the work. | [Read and adapt](shipshape/README.md) |

A workflow supplies guidance, not permissions or enforcement. Your instructions,
repository agreements and actual tools determine how it can be adopted.
