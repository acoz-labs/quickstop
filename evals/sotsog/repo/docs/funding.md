# Funding

Lanternhall's maintenance is funded by a three-year grant from the Halvorsen
Civic Foundation (2026 to 2028). The grant pays for the two maintainers' time.

## Conditions we must keep

From the grant agreement, section 4.2, "Software quality":

> Every change to the production codebase must be reviewed by a person other
> than its author before it is deployed, and the review must be recorded in a
> form the Foundation can audit.

The Foundation audits a sample of merged changes every year. We meet the
condition through required pull request reviews on `main`; the approving
review on each pull request is the record. Losing the grant would end paid
maintenance.
