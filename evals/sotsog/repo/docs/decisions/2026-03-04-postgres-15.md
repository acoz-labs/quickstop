# PostgreSQL 15: adopt for Lanternhall

We move production from PostgreSQL 12 to 15 in April. The IT team schedules
the upgrade; nothing else changes for contributors.

Date 2026-03-04, decided by both maintainers.

## Why

PostgreSQL 12 leaves support in November 2026, and 15 is what the IT team
already runs for the catalogue system.

## Revisit when

PostgreSQL 15 nears its own end of support (November 2027).
