# How 40 small teams write release notes: survey results

Source: https://harbourline.example.org/research/release-notes-survey-2026
Author: Harbourline Research Collective (an independent, volunteer-run group with no product to sell)
Published: 2026-05-30

We asked 40 teams of 2 to 15 developers how they produce release notes. 17 use
per-change fragment files checked in CI; 12 write notes by hand at release
time; 11 generate them from commit messages. Teams using fragments were the
most likely to say their notes were complete (15 of 17) and on time (14 of 17).
Teams generating from commits most often said their notes were too technical
for users (8 of 11). Self-reported, not measured.
