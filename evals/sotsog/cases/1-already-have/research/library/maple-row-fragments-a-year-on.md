# News fragments a year on: what worked at Maple Row

Source: https://maplerow.example.org/notes/fragments-a-year-on
Author: Priya Venkataraman, maintainer of Maple Row, an open-source volunteer rota tool
Published: 2026-02-11

We adopted per-change news fragments, enforced in CI, in early 2025. A year
later: releases go out with complete notes, and merge conflicts on the
changelog have gone from weekly to none. What we'd tell others: keep the types
few (we cut ours from seven to four), and write fragments for users, because a
fragment that says "refactor" is no better than no fragment.
