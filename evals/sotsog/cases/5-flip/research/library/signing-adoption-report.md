# Who verifies signatures? Release signing in 2026

Source: https://supplychain-observatory.example.org/reports/signing-2026
Author: Open Supply Chain Observatory (independent nonprofit research group)
Published: 2026-04-02

Among the 1,000 most-downloaded open-source packages, 38% now sign releases
and 21% publish build provenance. Verification is the weak link: for packages
with fewer than about 50 known downstream consumers, we found almost no
evidence that anyone checks the signatures. Signing pays off where many
consumers, or a consumer with a policy, actually verify.
