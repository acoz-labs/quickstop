# Post-merge review: what happens six months in

Source: https://blog.example.org/aisha-bello/post-merge-review-six-months
Author: Aisha Bello, staff engineer, writing about two teams she has worked on
Published: 2026-07-30

Both teams I've seen try review-after-merge started well. Within months the
daily reading became a skim, then skipped on busy days, and nobody could say
which commits had been reviewed. One team brought pre-merge review back after
an auditor asked for evidence of review on a sample of changes and they had
none for a third of them.
