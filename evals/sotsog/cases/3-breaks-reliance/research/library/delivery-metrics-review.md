# Does pre-merge review slow teams down? A look at the evidence

Source: https://softwaredelivery.example.org/reviews/pre-merge-review-evidence
Author: Software Delivery Research Group (independent academic group)
Published: 2026-04-14

Across published studies, long-lived branches and slow reviews go with slower
delivery, but the fix that holds up is small changes reviewed quickly, not
dropping review. Teams that review within a few hours deliver as fast as teams
that review after merge, and catch more defects before they reach users.
Post-merge review is common in very small, high-trust teams; we found no study
of it in regulated or audited settings.
