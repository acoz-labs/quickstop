# Pairing as review: getting a second pair of eyes without the wait

Source: https://devpractice.example.com/pairing-as-review
Author: Grace Lindgren, coach and author on team practices
Published: 2026-02-19

If waiting for review is the problem, review while you write: pair or mob on
the change, and record both names on the commit. Several teams treat a paired
commit as reviewed. Whether that satisfies an outside auditor depends on what
they accept as a record.
