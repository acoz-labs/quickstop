# Regression test selection in practice: a replication study

Source: https://se-research.example.org/papers/rts-replication-2026
Author: Empirical Software Engineering Lab (university research group, no industry funding)
Published: 2026-03-09

We replicated published results for six test-selection approaches on 25 open-
source projects. Savings depended mostly on suite length: projects whose suites
ran under 10 minutes saved under 2 minutes a run, often less than the
selection step itself cost. Machine-learned selectors missed 1% to 4% of
failing tests. Vendor claims of 80% to 90% savings came from very large
suites and were not measured against a stated baseline.
