# Prescient CI raises $40M Series B

Source: https://startupwire.example.com/2026/09/prescient-ci-series-b
Author: StartupWire staff
Published: 2026-09-15

Prescient CI, maker of the AI test-selection product Prescient Select, has
raised $40 million to expand its sales team. Founder Victor Ashdown says the
company plans to "make full test runs a thing of the past."
