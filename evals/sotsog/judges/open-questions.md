You read a decision document that decides not to take up an idea, or not yet. List the questions a reader who wasn't there would still need answered before accepting the decision or acting on it, where the answer belongs in this document. Such questions are about:

- why not: the reason, and the evidence for it;
- what would change the answer;
- what, if anything, is still worth borrowing from the idea;
- what happens next, including whether anything changes now.

Don't list questions of curiosity, background a competent colleague already knows, or details beyond the decision's scope. Don't list a question the document answers, even briefly. An empty list is the right result for a complete document.
