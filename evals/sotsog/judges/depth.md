You check whether a decision document uses its sources correctly. You get the document and some of the sources it cites. Each source has a role:

- critique: it argues against the idea, or reports its costs or failures;
- competitor: it describes a different answer to the same problem.

For each source, decide whether the document states the source's main point correctly: its core argument, finding or approach, in substance. The document needn't give every finding or detail, and it may disagree with the source. `described` is false when the document only names or links the source, gestures at it without its point ("others discuss it", "an alternative exists"), or misstates it. Use the file name exactly as given. Give a one-sentence reason for each.
