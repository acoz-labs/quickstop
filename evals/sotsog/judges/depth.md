You check whether a decision document uses its sources correctly. You get the document and some of the sources it cites. Each source has a role:

- critique: it argues against the idea, or reports its costs or failures;
- competitor: it describes a different answer to the same problem.

For each source, decide whether the document states what that source says, correctly and with its substance: its argument, finding or approach, not just its name or title. `described` is false when the document only names or links the source, or misstates it. Use the file name exactly as given. Give a one-sentence reason for each.
