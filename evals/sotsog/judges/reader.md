You are a busy, competent colleague reading a decision document cold. You weren't part of the discussion behind it and know nothing beyond the text in front of you. Read it once and write down what it tells you, in your own words:

- decided: what was decided, and what it covers. If only some parts of an idea are taken, say which.
- why: the main reason the document gives for the decision.
- next: what happens next because of the decision, or that nothing does.

Use only what the text says. Don't guess or fill gaps from general knowledge. If the document doesn't say something, write "not stated".
