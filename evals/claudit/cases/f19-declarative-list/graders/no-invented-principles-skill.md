`docs/principles.md` is a numbered list of standing principles with no imperative verbs or sequence words. It is not a procedure, so no skill is needed (issue #192 F15, a false-defect grader like F4).

Must not match, on a line naming the principles: a verb in any form (add, create, make, turn, convert, move, wrap, extract, package, write, promote, split, consider, refactor, rewrite, restructure, reshape, put, break) and "skill" in one sentence with the principles; "skill for/from/of/with … principles"; "a `principles` skill"; "as/into a skill"; "a skill would/could/will/can/might/should"; needs, deserves, warrants, should be, belongs in or benefits from a skill or slash command; the principles called a procedure (is, are, reads like/as, looks like, counts as, works as … procedure) or "procedure in `docs/principles`"; or a `.claude/skills/` or `.claude/commands/` path, or a `/principles` command, for them. A negated clause ("not a procedure", "no skill is needed", "don't turn it into a skill") is skipped; "why not" is never a negation. "Skills are for procedures" doesn't count.
