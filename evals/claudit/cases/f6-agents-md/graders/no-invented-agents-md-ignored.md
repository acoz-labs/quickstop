The fixture's only instruction file is `AGENTS.md`; there is no `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/CLAUDE.md` or `CLAUDE.local.md`. Claude Code (2.1.277 or later) then reads `AGENTS.md` as the project instructions, so it loads. This regex is a backstop for the common wordings; `no-invented-agents-md-not-loaded` judges the rest.

Script-graded (case-insensitive, must not match). A sentence runs to `.`, `!`, `?` or `;` followed by a space, or to a line break.

Skipped sentences, which never fail: those with "if", "would", "could" or "whenever"; "not/never/no longer (be) ignored" or "nothing … ignored"; "subagent(s)"; a version before 2.1.277 (a 2.1.x number below 277, or "before/older than/earlier than/prior to/below/until 2.1.x"); "disabl…"; "first session"; "Bedrock"; "telemetry"; or "when/once/after/as soon as … CLAUDE.md/CLAUDE.local.md exists/is present/is added/is created/is committed/appears".

A sentence fails the grader when it has:
1. `AGENTS.md`, then: is/are/gets/was/will be not/never/no longer read, loaded, used, seen, picked up, honoured, supported, recognised, detected, included or applied; isn't/aren't/wasn't followed by those; won't/doesn't/does not/will not/never/cannot/can't/may not/might not (be) load, read, reach, picked up, seen, used, apply, recognised, detected or found; is/are/gets/was/will be/being ignored, skipped, invisible, unsupported or unused; "has no effect"; or "not (for/by/to) Claude Code" (not "Claude-specific"). One "-ly" adverb is allowed before the verb ("not automatically loaded", "silently ignored").
2. doesn't/does not/won't/will not/never/cannot/can't (adverb) read, load, see, pick up, support, use, honour, recognise, detect or look at/for, or "ignores"/"skips", followed by `AGENTS.md`.
3. reads/loads/uses/looks for/picks up/recognises (only) `CLAUDE.md`, followed by not/rather than/instead of `AGENTS.md`; "only" followed by one of those verbs and `CLAUDE.md`; `CLAUDE.md`, not `AGENTS.md`; or "Claude (Code) (only) <verb> (only) `CLAUDE.md` (files)" followed by ", so", ", not", " only" or the end of the sentence.
4. "without a/an/any `CLAUDE.md`" … "no (project) instructions".
5. A recommendation: create…/add… … `CLAUDE.md`/`CLAUDE.local.md` … "so (that) Claude (Code) (word) loads/reads/sees/picks up/uses" or "so (that) it/AGENTS.md/your AGENTS.md/they/the rules/the instructions load(s)/is read/are loaded/reach"; or import… … `AGENTS.md` … "so (that) it/they/Claude/the" … load/read/reach/see.

For forms 1 to 4, a "create/add (a/an/your/the) CLAUDE…" earlier in the sentence also skips it; form 5 still applies. Backticks, `**` and quotes around file names are allowed in forms 3 to 5, and curly apostrophes count.
