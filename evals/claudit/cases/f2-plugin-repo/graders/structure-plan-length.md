The report follows Claudit's plan-length rule (`plugins/claudit/skills/claudit/SKILL.md`, step 5): under a `## Top changes` (or `###`) heading, 3 to 7 top-level numbered items, or a first line "N changes are worth making: <reason>." with N of 0 to 2 (also "Only 1 change is", "No changes are" or "Fewer than 3 changes are", then `:`, `.`, `;`, `,`, a dash or "because" and at least 10 characters of reason on the same line, optionally bold) followed by at most 2 items (the count itself isn't compared). A section whose first line claims 0 to 2 can't pass with 3 or more items. A count line for 3 or more is allowed but not checked. A top-level item is a line that starts at column 0 with `N.` or `N)`, optionally bold (`**1.`) or as a `###`/`####` heading. Indented lines, other lines and fenced code blocks (so numbered lines inside a fenced example don't count) belong to the current item; the section ends at the next `#` or `##` heading or the end of the report. A 4.0.1 target; other arms are expected to fail.
