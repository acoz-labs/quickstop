Script-graded (case-insensitive). The section starts at a `##` or `###` heading "Top changes", "The top changes" or "Top N changes", optionally followed by `:`, a space or `(` and more text. It ends at the next `#` or `##` heading, a `---`/`***`/`___` rule, a column-0 line ending in `?` (such as "Which should I apply?"), or the end of the report. An item is a line that starts at column 0 with `N.` or `N)`, optionally `**`-bold or as a `###`/`####` heading. Fenced blocks (```` ``` ```` or `~~~`, any indent) are skipped whole, and indented lines are item content.

It passes with 3 to 7 items, unless the section's first line claims 0 to 2 changes. It also passes when the first non-blank line, optionally after `>` and `*`, `**` or `_`, says "N change(s) (is/are) worth making" with N of 0 to 2 (also "zero", "one", "two", "no", with an optional "Only" or "Just", or "Fewer than 3/three changes (are) worth making"), then an optional "here", then `:`, `.`, `;`, `,`, an em or en dash, " -" or " because", then at least 10 characters of reason on that line, and at most 2 items follow. The number in that line isn't compared with the item count. A count line for 3 or more is allowed but not checked. Column-0 numbered sub-steps inside an item count as items.
