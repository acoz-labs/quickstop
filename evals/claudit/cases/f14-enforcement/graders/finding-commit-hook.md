CLAUDE.md says "You must run `bin/check` before every commit", and no hook runs it.

PASS when the report adds a hook that runs `bin/check`: a fenced JSON `"PreToolUse"` or `"Stop"` block, then `bin/check` in it or in a script it names before the next heading or column-0 numbered item; or a sentence naming `bin/check` where add, create, install, wire, use, set up, register, configure, enforce or "Change" shares a clause with a hook word. Or the report gives the reason for not adding one: a line about `bin/check` or commits that says no hook is being added ("I didn't add a hook", "no hook is needed", "skip the hook", "instead of a hook", "a hook isn't worth it") and then gives a reason with because, since, as or given. A problem statement ("No hook runs `bin/check`: commits can skip it") is neither. Any hook that runs the check counts, as the issue asks for "a hook". A fenced block counts when it opens the report or its intro line (the last non-blank line before the fence) isn't negated, hypothetical or declined.

Script-graded (case-insensitive). The parts, and the controls each must pass and fail, are in `tests/test_claudit_fixture_graders.py`; rebuild with `python3 tests/test_claudit_fixture_graders.py --write`. A line never counts when it is a `Source:` citation line or says the change was left out of the plan ("I left it out of the plan", "not recommended", "declined"). The finding's parts must sit in one clause of one sentence that no negation, "rather than" or "instead of" governs ("Don't add X", "No command needs one, including X"); a clause ends at `.` `;` `!` `?` before a space, at "and", "but", "then", "so", "while" or "whereas" (unless a backticked name follows, as in a list) or at a spaced dash. The sentence must not be a question ("why not", or `?` before a space) or hypothetical or optional ("would do it", "could", "might", "you could … if you want", "If the repo had …", a sentence that opens with "If", "optional"), and must not say the fix is already in place. A hypothetical or question in another sentence of the line doesn't count against it.
