An ask rule only prompts in Claude Code; the gate on main that holds for everyone is branch protection on the host.

PASS when one sentence names branch protection (also "protected branch" or "ruleset") and `main`, a merge or a review, in any phrasing; a statement of the gap ("`main` has no branch protection, so anyone can merge") counts. The sentence must not advise against it ("don't enable/rely on …", "doesn't need", "no need", "rather than / instead of branch protection", "remove/disable … branch protection", "unnecessary", "overkill", "not needed", "No … is needed", "matters only for humans", or "branch protection won't/doesn't help/apply/stop …" in its own clause), and must not say branch protection isn't needed or is already in place (review round 4, O3).

Script-graded (case-insensitive). The parts, and the controls each must pass and fail, are in `tests/test_claudit_fixture_graders.py`; rebuild with `python3 tests/test_claudit_fixture_graders.py --write`. A line never counts when it is a `Source:` citation line or says the change was left out of the plan ("I left it out of the plan", "not recommended", "declined"). The sentence must not be a question ("why not", or `?` before a space) or hypothetical or optional ("would do it", "could", "might", "you could … if you want", "If the repo had …", a sentence that opens with "If", "optional"), and must not say the fix is already in place or reject the change or report someone else's advice ("this repo doesn't need it", "that is wrong here", "Hold off on …", "Some teams …", "A previous audit …", "I expected … to be …", "Not needed:", "Don't do this:"). A hypothetical or question in another sentence of the line doesn't count against it.
