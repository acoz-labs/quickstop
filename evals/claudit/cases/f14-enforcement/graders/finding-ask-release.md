CLAUDE.md says a release needs a human go-ahead before `bin/release` runs, and nothing asks.

PASS when the report adds an ask rule for `Bash(bin/release` (or `./bin/release`), in a fenced `"ask"` array or a recommending sentence, as for `finding-ask-merge`.

Script-graded (case-insensitive). The parts, and the controls each must pass and fail, are in `tests/test_claudit_fixture_graders.py`; rebuild with `python3 tests/test_claudit_fixture_graders.py --write`. A line never counts when it is a `Source:` citation line or says the change was left out of the plan ("I left it out of the plan", "not recommended", "declined"). The finding's parts must sit in one clause of one sentence that no negation, "rather than" or "instead of" governs ("Don't add X", "No command needs one, including X"); a clause ends at `.` `;` `!` `?` before a space, at "but", "then", "so", "while" or "whereas", at "and" (unless a backticked name follows, as in a list), at a spaced dash, or at a colon before an imperative ("…, but nothing enforces it: add X"). The sentence must not be a question ("why not", or `?` before a space) or hypothetical or optional ("would do it", "could", "might", "you could … if you want", "If the repo had …", a sentence that opens with "If", "optional"), and must not say the fix is already in place or reject the change or report someone else's advice ("this repo doesn't need it", "that is wrong here", "Hold off on …", "Some teams …", "A previous audit …", "I expected … to be …", "Not needed:", "Don't do this:"). A hypothetical or question in another sentence of the line doesn't count against it.
