# Merchant payout terms (excerpt), section 7: delays

7.1 We aim to send each payout on the business day after the funds settle.

7.2 If a payout is late because of a fault on our side, a merchant may claim
reimbursement of bank fees the delay caused. Claims are made through merchant
support with evidence of the fee.

7.3 Reimbursement is at our discretion. Merchant support may approve claims up
to GBP 1,000 in total per incident. Above that, the head of payments operations
approves the incident's claims together.

7.4 We reply to every claim within 7 calendar days of filing, with either a
reimbursement or a reason for declining.
