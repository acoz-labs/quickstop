# Photo upload redesign

The app should upload photos straight to storage with short-lived signed links and send only references when it submits, so big inspections stop timing out at the gateway. Should we force everyone onto app 4.12 on 31 January, or keep the old upload path for older apps? Forcing it lets us shut the old path and its servers down but locks out inspectors whose phones can't run 4.12; keeping it means paying for both.
